Publications

My scholarly work focuses on water resources engineering, urban stormwater systems, green stormwater infrastructure, hydrologic and hydraulic modeling, geospatial analysis, and data-driven methods for infrastructure performance and resilience.

My research integrates field monitoring, process-based modeling, GIS, and machine learning to better understand stormwater-system performance, flooding, infrastructure condition, and resilience in urban and coastal environments.
